# Overview

`WHO 18` defines the OpenWebNet Energy Management system. Its established functional vocabulary includes power measurements, energy totalizers, energy-management actuator operations, Stop&Go functions, and historical energy data.

See [`dimensions.md`](dimensions.md) for the established `DIMENSION` families.

`WHO 18` is distinct from [`WHO 3`](../who-3-load-management/) Load Management and [`WHO 11`](../who-11-energy-distribution/) Energy distribution; their numeric protocol fields are not interchangeable.
