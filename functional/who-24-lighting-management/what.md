# `WHAT` Reference

Published `WHO 24` `WHAT` forms are parameterized rather than a simple scalar command table.

| `WHAT` form | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `1#PROFILE_ID` | Profile frame |
| `2#[0-1]` | Slave offset enable/disable |

These forms are part of the Lighting Management grammar and should be preserved as parameterized `WHAT` values. They are not equivalent to `WHO 1` Lighting `WHAT` values.
