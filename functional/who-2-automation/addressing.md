# Addressing

Automation shares the A/PL address-rule family used by Lighting. The MyHOME_Suite `OPEN.db` address-rule definitions represent point-to-point `[A][PL]`, environment `[A]`, and advanced address forms through system-specific rules.

A numeric `WHERE` must therefore be interpreted according to the Automation address rule in use rather than assumed to be one universal address form. See [`../../protocol/addressing.md`](../../protocol/addressing.md).
