# Protocol

`WHO 3` is the Load Management namespace. Its `WHAT` values represent load-management commands and states, while `WHERE` identifies the managed load or load-management target according to the `WHO 3` addressing rules.

The canonical public corpus contains a dedicated `WHO 3` specification. The current MyHOME Suite corpus also identifies Load Management as a functional system. Detailed value tables should be taken from those system-specific definitions rather than inferred from Lighting or Energy Management.

Energy measurements and the later energy-management `DIMENSION` model belong to [`WHO 18`](../who-18-energy-management/), not `WHO 3`.
