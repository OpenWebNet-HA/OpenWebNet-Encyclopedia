# Protocol

`WHO 5` is the Alarm namespace. The published protocol is primarily monitoring-oriented and defines alarm-system states and events together with system-specific addressing.

The public `WHO 5` specification is the canonical source for the published command/event vocabulary. The current MyHOME Suite corpus confirms `WHO 5` as a separate functional namespace but does not justify extending the older published `WHAT` vocabulary with inferred meanings.

Accordingly, undocumented numeric values must remain unspecified until supported by the implementation corpus or observed wire behavior.
