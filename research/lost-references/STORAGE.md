# Lost-reference storage

Recovered third-party and standards-reference binaries are preserved in the private `openwebnet-references` bucket.

- Objects are content-addressed as `sha256/<first-2>/<next-2>/<sha256>`.
- Public repository metadata records bibliographic identity, discovery provenance, SHA-256, size, and the private object key.
- Private preservation does not imply redistribution rights.
- Claims derived from a restricted artifact should carry a precise page, clause, table, or equivalent locator.
- Short source excerpts may be recorded where useful for verification, subject to quotation limits.
- The private object key is an integrity and preservation locator, not a public download URL.
