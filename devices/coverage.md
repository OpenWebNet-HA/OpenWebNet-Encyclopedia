# Device Coverage

This index tracks the current state of product-specific documentation and research.

It is both a navigation aid and a research backlog. A missing or incomplete entry does not mean that a product is unsupported by OpenWebNet.

Coverage is tracked by technical Device definition rather than by choosing one commercial SKU as canonical. The [Complete Device Index](index.md) provides the exhaustive brand / SKU lookup.

Semantic acceptance is tracked in the [work queue](work-queue.md) and the [0001–0010](../project/review/device-reviews-0001-0010-2026-10-05.md) and [0011–0020](../project/review/device-reviews-0011-0020-2026-10-05.md) and [0021–0030](../project/review/device-reviews-0021-0030-2026-10-06.md) and [0031–0040](../project/review/device-reviews-0031-0040-2026-10-06.md) and [0041–0050](../project/review/device-reviews-0041-0050-2026-10-06.md) and [0051–0060](../project/review/device-reviews-0051-0060-2026-10-06.md) and [0061–0070](../project/review/device-reviews-0061-0070-2026-10-06.md) and [0071–0080](../project/review/device-reviews-0071-0080-2026-10-06.md) and [0081–0090](../project/review/device-reviews-0081-0090-2026-10-06.md) and [0091–0100](../project/review/device-reviews-0091-0100-2026-10-06.md) and [0101–0110](../project/review/device-reviews-0101-0110-2026-10-06.md) and [0111–0120](../project/review/device-reviews-0111-0120-2026-10-06.md) and [0121–0130](../project/review/device-reviews-0121-0130-2026-10-06.md) and [0131–0140](../project/review/device-reviews-0131-0140-2026-10-06.md) and [0141–0150](../project/review/device-reviews-0141-0150-2026-10-06.md) and [0151–0160](../project/review/device-reviews-0151-0160-2026-10-07.md) and [0161–0170](../project/review/device-reviews-0161-0170-2026-10-07.md) and [0171–0180](../project/review/device-reviews-0171-0180-2026-10-07.md) and [0181–0190](../project/review/device-reviews-0181-0190-2026-10-07.md) and [0191–0200](../project/review/device-reviews-0191-0200-2026-10-07.md) review records. ## Coverage fields

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Database extraction | Device-specific identity, firmware, Module/Object, configuration, conditions, and constraints available from canonical implementation sources have been curated |
| Commercial identities | Known brand / SKU identities and package/synonym relationships are documented |
| Official sources | Authoritative product documents are inventoried, archived where appropriate, and reconciled into the Device dossier; unreconciled known sources keep this field Partial |
| Identity | Protocol/catalogue identity mapping is documented |
| Hardware evidence | At least one observation from known physical hardware exists |
| Functions | Modules, Objects, roles, and relevant functional systems are documented |
| Configuration | Supported configuration methods and parameter domains are documented |
| Constraints | Material configuration ranges, conditions, conversion constraints, and source irregularities are documented |

Use **Complete**, **Partial**, **Unknown**, or **Not applicable** where a simple yes/no would hide important gaps.
