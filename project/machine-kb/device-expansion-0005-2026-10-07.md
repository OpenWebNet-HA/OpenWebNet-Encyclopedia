# Accepted Device Machine KB expansion, batch 5

This batch migrates twenty accepted Device definitions, OWN-DEV-0066 through OWN-DEV-0086 excluding the already integrated pilot device OWN-DEV-0076. The inventory has 90 integrated and 120 pending definitions. All eight actual acceptance checks remain required by the closed Device inventory. This migration does not independently recertify Device descriptions or authorize a release.

## Conservation and provenance

All 3,080 prepared source units are accounted for: 2,892 claimed and 188 reviewed nonclaim units. Documentation and source navigation retain their corpus/chunk context and registered-source provenance. No material configuration or governing prose is dismissed as navigation.

The batch adds 3,700 claims, including 26 separate attributable assertions in thirteen reciprocal conflict pairs, and 782 scoped configuration entities. Complete commercial/system relationships, firmware/build tuples, Module/Object/Virgin placements, fields, domains, defaults, conditions, filters, conversions, modes, connections, parameters, packages and governing qualifications are preserved. Domains and defaults remain separate, with exact source-unit keys and fingerprints for their atomicity and evidence reviews.

45 existing registered originals are reused; 38 gain new stable KB source references. Manifest SHA-256 and byte-length records bind the retained originals. No original or PDF was newly added. Linked parameter, software, firmware and unavailable document payloads remain unexamined. Filenames and catalogue associations do not establish payload contents or installed behavior. SKU-to-item relationships establish catalogue identities independently of missing PDFs.
