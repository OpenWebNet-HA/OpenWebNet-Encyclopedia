Inspection scope is the applicable exact-product material and claims, not a certification of every unrelated product or screenshot in multi-product documents. English/French sensor and actuator variants, historical/regional Arnould material, all identified F454 English manual revisions and exact EAN records are retained separately. Page-image checks covered ambiguous Arnould packaging/ratings, the Spanish load table, instruction drawings and touch-control key-count context.
