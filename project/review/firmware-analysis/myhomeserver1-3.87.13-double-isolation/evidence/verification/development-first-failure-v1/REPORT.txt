## Exact source and ABI premises

The provider declares ARM v7 application/Thumb-2, VFPv3/NEON and `Tag_ABI_VFP_args: VFP registers`, with IEEE754 number-model attributes. Those are packaged ABI declarations, not target execution. The selected constructor takes the supplied scalar in D0 and copies it through D8, with its receiver in R0. Stack/receiver validity, ordinary hard-float callee preservation and effective candidate self-binding must remain explicit experiment premises.

Selected source entries: C1(double) `0x15ce0`; initBasic `0x15ad4`; Comments move `0x159ec`; type `0x14e3c`; asDouble `0x171c4`, its selected type3 target `0x1724e` and normal epilogue `0x171fe`. A harness must not pre-seed the resulting type or union in place of those original instructions.

Original Comments move loads the source pointer, zeros the source slot and installs that pointer at the destination. Since the actual constructor first wrote destination+12=0 and initBasic supplies a local zero source, its selected old-destination-null branch at `0x159fa` reaches `0x15a1e` without the nonnull deallocation calls. The unused nonnull paths must remain disallowed/abort boundaries rather than successful host-free substitutes. type() reads object+8; the original TBB bytes `37 18 1f 34 3a 03` select `0x1724e` for type3. That block reads the union into D0 and returns through the original normal stack-guard epilogue.

The three selected self-definition requests are original ARM PLT entries `0xba94` (initBasic), `0xb074` (Comments move), and `0xb768` (type), with original JUMP_SLOT definitions `0x15ad5`, `0x159ed`, `0x14e3d`. Any explicit redirection in the proposed experiment must call those original candidate bodies and preserve link/Thumb state; it is not a proof of the installed loader's interposition or binding decisions. No scalar/body shortcut or provider-success primitive is justified by this packet.
