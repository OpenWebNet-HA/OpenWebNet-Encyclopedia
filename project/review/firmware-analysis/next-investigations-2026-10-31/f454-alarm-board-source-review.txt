## Predecessor/ordinal premises and limits

I inspected the additional original register-port prologue0xc0187254..0xc0187280 before the retained selection span. Thus clock/provider/entry success is an explicit additional local prerequisite; the report's BASI record has a nonzero clock, but no hardware or provider outcome is inferred.

The subsequent code first checks matching existing ports, then type/IObase-zero slots, with a further untyped-slot fallback. A platform ID or declaration order is not itself a tty ordinal. The report's finite sufficient premise correctly requires typed DM365 slots0/1, available slot2, no earlier match/other registration/override changing that state, and successful BASI probe/register completion. Under those premises slot2/base minor64 gives minor66. The review confirms that conditional composition; it does not establish those premises actually hold during boot or eliminate other mappings.

The kernel source does not close the net/connector relationship between the BASI address0x04000000/interrupt65 and the documented SCS AI terminal. Driver/table/tty names and ordinal arithmetic are insufficient electrical wiring evidence.
