Their **lines103–106** sort/source `/etc/init.d/scripts/S*`; **lines120–126** enumerate regular `/etc/applications/*`, fork and source each selected script in the child. These are Tcl source control requests under ordinary interpreter/helper/file/provider premises, not observed boot execution. STARTUP.json retains exact safe source lines and hashes. A full init/respawn/error policy is not reconstructed.

No HTTP/authentication/session or server body is examined or executed.

The natural caller of this wrapper, `/bin/bt_start`'s body, application extraction/copy and successful launch remain **OPEN**. Do not compose the script filename or XML process list into an unproved complete boot-to-application chain. The selected `cp_tmp.tcl` line names a copy source/target only, not a live data copy.
