## Consumer connection and limits

The same plugin requests `g_get_real_time` at `0xc650` and saves returned low/high words to SL/FP at `0xc660/0xc664`. In the selected sound/run23 utility path, the later `send_util_state` call precedes the deadline write. At `0xcc8c` the immediate is 600; `0xcc90` adds it to saved SL with carry, `0xcc98` propagates carry into saved FP, and `0xcc9c/0xcca0` store low/high words to the supplied private context at offsets 152/156. These are entry-clock words, not a fresh clock sampled at the later store. The preserved original words and spans support this connection; the earlier sound report and its independent review are hashed read-only inputs.

This resolves the unit of the arithmetic increment only under the provider/ABI/success premises.
