## Material result

Exact selector **`SYSDATE`** calls **time→gmtime**, unlike CHKTIME's previously observed time→localtime. Empty year followed by `#`, empty month followed by `*`, and empty day followed by `*` preserve the corresponding current component. This is exact selected delimiter/field behavior, not a complete accepted date/OPEN/DSL format or a Boolean scenario condition.
