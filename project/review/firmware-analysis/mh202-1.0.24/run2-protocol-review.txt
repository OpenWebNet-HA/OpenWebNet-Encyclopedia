## Original route and prerequisite checks

I inspected the original selected `libdiag`, `libopen` and `libwhere` spans, including the member-pointer relocation and PIC literals:

- `openKeyGen` constructs the local object at `SP+4`, invokes `CreateMsgOpen`, then reads kind at `SP+0x6240`, establishing object-relative offset `0x623c`. The report correctly selects the `libopen` candidate layout rather than importing `libcommon`'s offset.
- The selected kind-4 branch extracts DIM, calls `unpackKey`, and prefixes `R`. The no-`#` and parameter-count suffix branches support the host reconstruction `0` to `R0P0`; this is not an executed native parser fixture. The synthetic full frame's successful parsing and WHERE fields remain assumptions.
- The General constructor's `R0P0` entry copies the two-word member pointer from the table slot `0x380c8`; its `R_ARM_ABS32` relocation targets `o2s_allParameters` at `0x15b60`. That method assigns `8C000100` and returns numeric zero in its response-type namespace. This establishes the selected entry, not the unresolved outer registry/factory selection or collisions.
- General `open2scs` invokes the selected member and stores the result before calling `getScsAddr` at `0x191b0`. This precedes the WHO1000 test. The outer WHERE helper extracts WHO and looks up its map, with an exception path for a missing key. The report correctly leaves map initialization and selected WHERE1 translation unresolved.
- Only after that prerequisite does the WHO1000 compare/branch choose literal `$04`, bypassing the generic header call. The continuation assembles header and method suffix and pushes a string into the output vector. Thus `$048C000100` is a conditional internal ASCII candidate, not serial bytes, a send operation or a completed transaction.

The stated conclusion is appropriately narrow: absence from a generic WHO-to-SCS map alone cannot settle a separate special General branch; existence of that branch alone cannot prove WHO1000 reachability. The saved instruction sequence does not justify promoting WHERE1 or the full synthetic frame to accepted input. The report preserves those limits and distinguishes parser kind, response-type and SCS selector namespaces.
