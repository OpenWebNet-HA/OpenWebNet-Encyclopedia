## Scenario engine and WHO 17

Original `bt_taskmanager` has a WHO 17 post-parser dispatcher at `0xa72c`, with its ordinary `WHAT` jump table at `0xa7dc..0xa7e8`. These are original ELF virtual addresses, scoped to the exact file hash in the [functional report](../../project/review/firmware-analysis/mh202-1.0.24/run1-functional-report.txt).

| `WHAT` | Selected native operation | Retained boundary |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| `1` | Launch helper `0xd4f8` | Existing record, nonzero WHERE and additional state/global guards apply |
| `2` | Stop helper `0xd47c` | Selected record or general-WHERE iteration; PID/FIFO policy is separate |
| `3` | Enable helper `0xd2f4` | Changes the record's disabled marker |
| `4` | Disable helper `0xd258` | Changes the record's disabled marker |

Two native renderers format `*17*%d*%d##`: `0xcad0` chooses availability `3/4` from record offset `+52`, while `0xcb04` chooses execution/control `2/1` from `+60`. Nonzero control state includes a stop-request state; it must not automatically become proof of a physically running script. Complete publisher/subscription/transport ownership remains open.

The loader at `0xce14..0xd198` searches numeric files `1..300` using `home/bticino/cfg/extra/4/<number>.spt/<number>.spt`, continuing across failed opens. It binds `$TRIGGERSTART`, `$TRIGGERSTOP`, `$TRIGGERENABLE` and `$TRIGGERDISABLE` to four actual callbacks. Launch constructs a `bt_script` command and two FIFO paths. These are concrete loader/call sites, not successful process creation, a complete script grammar or a proven trigger matcher.

The first batch located `$RESTART`, `SEMPRE`, a global command gate and interpreter operations. The [state/routing continuation](mh202-1.0.24-state-and-routing.md) adds concrete event/callback, configured panic-frame, FIFO/PID, saved-state restoration and partial `IIF` joins. Their names do not yet establish the complete manual-described only-if/restart/panic lifecycle. The absent configured `.spt` bodies are an explicit evidence gap. Selected COSO scenario translation now has [body/list evidence](mh202-1.0.24-state-and-routing.md#coso-scenario-translation-is-separate-from-scheduling), while its state/application/timer paths remain separate from this WHO 17 scheduler.
