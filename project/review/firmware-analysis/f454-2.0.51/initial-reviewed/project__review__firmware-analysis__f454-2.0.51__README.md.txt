## Reproduction limits

The capsule does not include original images, vendor binaries, private Configuration, the full harness or all saved handoffs. Original-instruction execution cannot be reproduced from these excerpts alone. An authorized researcher needs the exact registered archive and full original research dependencies; real hardware/runtime outcomes require additional evidence.

No credential/private-key material or real gateway contact was used in this curation. Synthetic pointers, descriptors and protocol examples must retain their synthetic status. Do not classify these records as complete dynamic-oracle results or physical observations.
