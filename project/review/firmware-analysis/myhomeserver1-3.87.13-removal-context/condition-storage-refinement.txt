# Scoped condition-storage terminology refinement

The immutable prior owner-removal report used the phrase `Trigger+104/condition+116 controls` in its final namespace paragraph. Its documentary review used `trigger/condition handles`. Exact complete prior files, hashes and quotes are retained in integration/condition-storage-prior/INDEX.json. Those phrases were too broad if read as a claim that the condition range shares the trigger/action two-word control-record layout. The prior numeric-tree removal/pair-clear/count/disposal/erase evidence is unchanged.

Fresh selected source separates these representations. Original initializer0xa7a1e stores a compiled condition primary pointer in a one-word temporary; its normal call to0xa7264 publishes toward scene+116. The available-capacity helper0xa726e..0xa7278 loads ONE word, stores it at the old end, advances end by4 and zeros ONE source word. The selected generic condition consumer takes a condition pointee from that range and invokes its primary vtable+8. This is a4-byte pointer entry on the retained normal path, separate from scene+104 trigger8-byte pointer/control records and scene+128 action8-byte pointer/control records. The new scene-condition source packet and independent review retain exact original bytes/identities.

This corrects a peripheral storage label; it does not turn the condition pointer into proof of absent ownership, classify all growth/destructor/error/alias paths, or establish lifetime adequacy/failure. The full-capacity delegate and complete condition ownership remain separate premises. No original execution, configured vector or runtime address is supplied. Prior reports/reviews/first copies and earlier correction history remain unchanged, with this new refinement explicitly linked in current synthesis/reference integration.
