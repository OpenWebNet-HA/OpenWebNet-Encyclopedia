## Strongest bounded matching anchors

Fresh EN_DEVICE aggregate queries count331 embedded rows and541 Suite rows. In each source exact `(code,id_brand,id_line)` has zero duplicate groups, zero missing/empty code rows and zero multiple-item ambiguity per key. EN_BRAND/EN_LINE are among the prior complete equal-row tables, making literal branded commercial identity a stronger within-pair anchor than assuming id_device/id_item stability. This uniqueness is an observed property of these two sources, not a declared universal natural-key contract or proof of stable semantics for every matched record.

Keep exact text/case/leading zeroes and SQLite storage types. Different branded SKUs can share an item; descriptive labels and family placeholders can be reused. EN_DEVICE.code is not EN_LANGUAGE.code. A stronger item match should retain its complete commercial alias set, main/nonmain AS_ITEM_SYSTEM associations, external modobj/system context and relationship bundle, while reporting conflicts or unmatched records explicitly.

Firmware comparison should carry resolved item/commercial ownership plus V/R, build/localization, status/default, declared slot count and Object alternatives. Explicit-1 version components, no EN_BUILDS row, Deprecated status and default flag remain different states. Same numeric id_firmware, id_key_object or child-association key alone does not prove semantic equality across versions.

For Object/configuration comparison preserve internal id_key_object versus external key_object; Virgin Object key space; AS_OBJECT_FIRMWARE owner; Device-local first_slot; slot conditions and configuration/filter/conversion context. Multiple EN_SLOTS alternatives are not extra Modules. EN_CONF's Object-scope(id_firmware0) versus firmware-scope(id_key_object0) ownership makes0 a not-applicable discriminator, not automatically an orphan. idx is context-local, and condition/filter values must not be evaluated with partial inputs. Duplicate rows in tables without appropriate unique keys remain multisets.

## High-value query cautions for author areas

Before interpreting additions/removals, separate raw PK event counts from stable-owner semantic matches and explicitly quantify reused/moved IDs. Match firmware/Object/configuration bundles after ownership is resolved, not merely labels. Retain build/status/default/localization and empty-versus-NULL differences. Test parent coverage with per-table sentinel/type policy; do not treat every zero/-1 or same-name column as a mandatory FK.

Check AS_OBJECT_VIRGIN_OBJECT, AS_SLOT_CONDITION, AS_OBJECT_COLLECTION and hidden configuration/filter associations in complete owner context. AS_FW_PACKAGE emptiness or removal does not prove a package never existed; code/resources may be supplied elsewhere. Visibility, hidden/read-only flags, rule applicability, capability and installed state are separate. Support rows retained for an absent commercial item or an absent root relation do not by themselves show a runtime error or hardware absence.

The embedded catalogue's previously scoped item2198/MyHOMEServer1 self-lookups are empty. Therefore the firmware-bundled catalogue is not presumed a complete current self-capability database or an authoritative replacement for the archived Suite profile. Its literal export_date is metadata, not established chronology. Equal schemas and version tuples do not establish an upgrade path.
