ANA_OGGETTI.ID_OBJ, ANA_FAMIGLIA.ID_FAMIGLIA, ANA_CHI.ID_CHI, ANA_COSA.ID_COSA, ANA_DOVE.ID_DOVE, area IDs and all conversion/request/field/discriminator IDs are local to this source. Catalogue key_object/Module slots, old ScenarioDevices ObjectId/FamilyId, firmware record offsets and these editor IDs also remain separate. Matching names, counts or source-language labels do not bridge them. Existing source policy requires a literal/provenanced mapping, explicit correlation or independently observed behavior, and functional grammar validation for any rendered frame. Symbolic/absent templates and CUSTOM paths must not be invented as literal frames.

The 24 empty TMP tables include a defined scenario-instance shape with SCEN_NAME/REPEAT/RESTART/SPT_PATH and stage/condition/action associations. That narrows known schema availability beyond the two old four-table catalogues, but there is NO configured instance in these shipped working tables. Empty tables do not prove no product feature or no generator; schemas do not prove runtime evaluation order, actual file generation or a full Start/OnlyIf/Stop/Action graph.
