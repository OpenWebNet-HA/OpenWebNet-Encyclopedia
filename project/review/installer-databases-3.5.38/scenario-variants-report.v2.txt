Its ordered fields are `#1`, `ID_DOVE`, `1` or `4`, `[101-200]*`, followed by empty fields. The literal range/pattern text is preserved exactly; no matcher, wildcard, payload-completeness or accepted-wire interpretation was executed.

The reviewed same-installer compiler source provides the relevant consumer context: DataManager.GetOPENRuleCompile selects by object, command and area flag; Core materializes all10 OPEN_FIELD properties; MakeOpenCommand appends nonempty ordered fields and substitutes ID_DOVE where present. Under compatible resource/provider and source-object premises, selecting PK284 therefore changes a literal field of the assembled trigger pattern. No concrete WHERE, user scenario or compiler output is fabricated, and upstream editor/resource binding remains unobserved.
